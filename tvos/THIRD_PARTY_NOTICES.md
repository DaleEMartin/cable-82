# Third-party notices

This app is a native tvOS port of **CABLE 82** (https://github.com/nothans/cable-82).
`CableCore/Sources/CableCore/Dial.swift`, `Schedule.swift`, and `Guide.swift` are translations of CABLE 82's
`dial.js` and parts of `config-schema.js`. `CableCore/Tests/CableCoreTests/Reference/` has verbatim copies of
those two files, taken at commit 5a91fec, which the tests use as the reference implementation.
